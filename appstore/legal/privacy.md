# Scroll Party Privacy Policy

*Draft. Last updated: November 4, 2026.*

Scroll Party is a party game. This policy explains what data it uses. Controller: Santi Legaristi. Contact: `CONTACT_EMAIL`.

## What we store
- **Nickname**, chosen by you.
- **Selfie**, optional, as a small image used as your avatar.
- **Video links and anonymous clues** you add to a party.
- **Votes and reactions** during a game.
- **An anonymous identifier** generated by Firebase. We do not ask for your email, phone or real name.

## Why
Only to run the game: showing players, the video list, votes and scores. Our legal basis is providing the service you request.

## Who can see it
- The other members of your party see your nickname, selfie, votes and reactions.
- Your links stay hidden until the game starts, and then only the host's device reads them.
- Your clues are shown to the party without your name.

We do not sell data, show ads or track you across apps.

## Service providers
Data is hosted on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), in the European Union multi-region `eur3`.
Videos open in their original apps, under those apps' own policies.

## How long we keep it
Party data currently stays stored until you ask us to delete it. Leaving a party removes it from your device only.

## Your rights
You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to `CONTACT_EMAIL` with your party code
and nickname. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

## Children
Scroll Party is not intended for children under 13.

## Changes
We will publish any change on this page.
