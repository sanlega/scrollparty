## Texto para pegar en «Notes»

Scroll Party is an in-person party game. No account is required.

The game needs at least two people. To test it you need two devices with the app:
1. Device A: skip the intro, take or choose a photo, enter a name and tap "Create a party". Note the 6-character code.
2. Device B: enter a name, tap "Join a party" and enter the code.
3. On either device, tap "Add a reel" and paste a public video link, for example https://youtu.be/dQw4w9WgXcQ
4. Device A (host): tap "Start the party".
5. Device A: tap the play button (the video opens in its own app). Device B: choose who sent it.
6. Device A: tap "Reveal who sent it", then continue until the final screen.

To test sharing: in another app, open a video, tap Share and choose ScrollParty while in a party.

The host's phone is meant to be mirrored to a TV with AirPlay. No AirPlay device is needed for review.

The app stores links only. It does not download, host or embed videos. Videos open in their original apps.
Parties are private and can only be joined with a code.
Selfies are used only as an avatar for the members of that party.

If you only have one device, contact us and we will prepare a party for you.
