Scroll Party turns the videos you send your friends into the best game of the night.

Everyone secretly drops their favourite reels in the machine. When you get together, the host mirrors their phone to the TV and you watch them one by one. Then comes the question that starts every argument: who sent that?

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a party and invite your friends with a code. No sign-up needed.
• Take a selfie. Your face goes on the reels.
• Add videos by pasting a link or sharing from your favourite app.
• Mirror the host's phone to the TV with AirPlay.
• Watch each video and pick your suspect on your own phone.
• Watch the reel spin and stop on the real sender.

WIN TICKETS
Guess right and you earn a ticket. Fool your friends and you earn one for every friend you fooled. At the end, the machine hands out the prizes: Top Detective, Master Bluffer and Reel of the Night.

MADE FOR THE ROOM
• Anonymous clues to throw everyone off the scent
• Live reactions that fly across the TV
• A secret vote for the host: nobody sees their pick on the big screen
• Nobody sees your videos until the party starts

Perfect for dinners, birthdays, flat parties, road-trip stops and any night that needs a little chaos.

Scroll Party only stores links: videos open in their original apps.
